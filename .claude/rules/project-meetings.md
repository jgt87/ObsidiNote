Moved word for word from AGENTS.md "Architecture" (2026-11-25); AGENTS.md points here. Same upkeep
as there: only what the code cannot show, changed in the same turn as the code.

- Today's meetings (D-064; after kit P10 on the target machine: a day's list works, "the last 7 days" came back empty): `lib/Meetings.psm1` (pure: kit P10's two questions plus `ref`, reply readers, `ConvertTo-MeetingNoteText`). Attendees (2026-11-18): only the people who joined (not every invitee: "as in the invitation" made Copilot list invitees), asked as `[{name, email}]` with the address as the invitation has it, "never guess one"; `Get-MeetingAttendees` also reads plain names and "Name <address>", keeps an address only when it looks like one, one person per address; the page lists "Name ([address](mailto:address))"; logs and the kit count addresses, never write one. `POST /api/meetings/day {day?}` (today on this PC when absent) and `POST /api/meetings/notes {title, start, time, day}` ask Copilot through Ask's adapter (`Send-MeetingQuestion`: a slow answer is stopped, nothing waits), are read only, log counts only, 503 without Copilot, 502 for an unusable answer; notes of another day are refused. The window (`ui-src/src/meetings/`, the home page's "Today's meetings", session state) files each ticked meeting through `POST /api/submit` with origin `meeting` (origin table + `tests/fixtures/origins.json`), and Add files origin `meeting` as type `meeting` whatever Copilot proposed (`Invoke-TextAdd`). Tests: `tests/Meetings.Tests.ps1`, `meetings/meetingsModel.test.ts`.
- Meetings of other days from the box (D-078): `lib/MeetingRequest.psm1` (pure, EN + NL) reads a short command into days + series; `Get-Intent` rule 5 (auto only, after the explicit choices) answers intent `meetings` with `meetings = {days, series, when, error}` and the reason for the intent line; `POST /api/submit` answers `{intent:"meetings", days, series, when}` at once (no Copilot, no vault, nothing written; 400 with the reason when it cannot be fetched). The window's Meetings panel then asks `POST /api/meetings/day {day, series}` once per day (never one question for several days: kit P10), oldest first; `series` filters server-side by `Test-MeetingInSeries` (the filter's words inside the series key; `total` = the day before filtering). Plural "meetings"/"vergaderingen", or (D-079) a verb at the start plus a time naming past days without the word ("Fetch yesterday", "Get Daily Standup this week"; at most 5 series words unless they read as a meeting title: a "|" or every word capitalised; "Get milk tomorrow" stays a note); phrasings are normalised first (`Convert-RequestText`: possessives, number and month words, short weekdays, "this morning", "whole week", commas, "I missed"), then read by the rules in `Find-WhenPhrase` (between, since, N days ago, two days, ranges, weeks, months, N weeks); without the plural word (bare verb, or singular "meeting") the series must pass `Test-TitleLike`; `tests/fixtures/meeting-request-samples.json` (162 phrasings, today 2031-04-11) is the contract, add a sample with every new phrasing; a named series without a time takes the past 7 days (workdays; D-080), no series and no time is today; "this weeks", "this week's", "the week" are this week; a series before the word needs a time or a verb, so notes such as "meeting with Ada yesterday" stay Adds; questions ("?", question words, "about", "with") stay Asks; a quoted series, or one after a marker ("with the topic of", "called", "met het onderwerp"; `$script:SeriesMarker`, 2026-11-20), may hold any words; a time may stand mid-sentence or before the word after a verb (`Find-WhenPhrase -Anywhere`; series up to 20 words after a verb, 8 without; text up to 250 characters). The day prompt also asks for meetings invited to but not joined (`joined` flag, "you did not join" in the list) and, with a series, names it ("title contains"); the notes prompt says the person may not have joined. Weeks are Monday to Friday, future days dropped, at most 14 days. Meetings carry `day` and `key` (`<day>#<i>`) in the window; ticks and row states use the key. Tests: `tests/MeetingRequest.Tests.ps1`, fixture samples with want `meetings`, `meetings/meetingsModel.test.ts`.
- Meeting series and Ask within one (D-068, D-069): `obsn.series` (the Teams title without a date) and `obsn.meetingAt` (yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm or a day) are page Meta, index fields (`sr`, `ma`), store and graph fields (`tests/fixtures/graph-pages.json`). Same occurrence = same series, same day, same time when both have one (`Test-MeetingSameOccurrence`); a second one that day is titled with its time. Fetched several times a day (2026-11-18): an Add of a meeting whose earlier Add waits in the Intake answers `waiting` with that item's id (store `WaitingTexts`, optional), a resumed Add whose meeting got its page meanwhile updates it and files the item; `POST /api/meetings/day` sets `inWiki`/`waiting` per meeting and the window does not tick those; new meeting pages get a write-once `notes` outline. Real names from the operator's data: gitignored `tasks/private-names.txt`, read by `tests/NoReferenceName.Tests.ps1`.
