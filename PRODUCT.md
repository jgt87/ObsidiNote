# Product — ObsidiNote

## Register
product  <!-- product = a tool people work in; brand = a marketing surface -->

## Users
One knowledge worker, daily, on a Windows PC where nothing can be installed. Captures things in passing (a few seconds per item) and comes back to explore or ask when preparing work.

## Purpose
Turn scattered information into a linked personal wiki without filing work. Success pattern: paste, glance at the proposed links, accept, leave; later: ask or explore the map, open the page in OneNote.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, trustworthy. The content and its connections are the only thing that stands out.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS styling: decorative gradients, glow, glass, stat-card grids.
- Neon "knowledge graph" visuals (glowing nodes on black, particle dust): pretty, unreadable, colour and dots as decoration. Every dot on our map is real content.
- Status pills such as "connected": noise.

## Design Principles
1. The user's content is the star; chrome stays quiet: greys, with one purple accent for action, selection and focus.
2. Show what the machine decided (topics, links, why) and make every decision undoable.
3. Every view works without Copilot except the parts that need it; say plainly when Copilot is busy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Keyboard-operable, visible `:focus-visible`, WCAG AA contrast, reduced-motion respected.
- Never encode meaning in colour alone.
